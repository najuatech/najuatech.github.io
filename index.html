Purpose · Progress · Possibility

Building the Future, Together.

We believe innovation begins with people who dare to learn, imagine, and create. Najua Tech welcomes young talent from every background.

◎

Our Vision

To become a leading platform for youth-driven technology innovation in Tanzania, inspiring and empowering the next generation of developers and innovators to build solutions that shape the future.

↗

Our Mission

To empower young developers and innovators from all backgrounds through practical challenges, collaboration, mentorship, and opportunities to transform their ideas into meaningful technology solutions.

The Challenge

Three months.
One vision.

Our proposed competition format moves from early selection stages to a final development challenge where outstanding talent can demonstrate what they can build.

Discover & Select

STAGE 02

Develop & Advance

STAGE 03

The Final Challenge

Finalists demonstrate their abilities through a development challenge.
