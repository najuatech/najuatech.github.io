The Challenge

Three months.
One vision.

Our proposed competition format moves from early selection stages to a final development challenge where outstanding talent can demonstrate what they can build.

STAGE 01

Discover & Select

The proposed first stage introduces participants and begins the elimination process.

STAGE 02

Develop & Advance

The proposed second stage narrows the field and challenges participants to progress.

STAGE 03

The Final Challenge

Finalists demonstrate their abilities through a development challenge.
